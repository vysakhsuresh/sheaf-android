Sheaf privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Sheaf for Android, by Layerbit AI

Sheaf collects no data. Nothing you open, scan or make in Sheaf leaves your phone, and nothing about you or how you use the app is sent anywhere.

No internet permission

Sheaf does not hold Android's internet permission, so it cannot open a network connection at all. You can check this on the app's Play Store listing under "About this app" → "App permissions". There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting service and no account.

Your files stay on your device

Every tool - merging, compressing, OCR, signing, converting, scanning - runs on the phone itself. Sheaf only reads files you choose through Android's own file picker, or that you share or open with it, and it writes results only where you tell it to save them. Working copies are kept in the app's private cache and can be cleared from the About screen at any time.

Camera

The camera is used only to photograph pages in the scanner, and only after you allow it. Photos are turned into a PDF on the device and are not uploaded or kept anywhere else.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Sheaf shows the progress of long jobs. They are created on the device and contain no information sent from or to anywhere.

Links that leave the app

Buttons such as "Rate Sheaf", "Buy us a coffee", WhatsApp or email hand a web address to another app on your phone, which opens it under its own privacy policy. No part of your documents goes with it.

Crash logs

If Sheaf crashes, a short technical log is saved in the app's private storage. It is never sent automatically. You can choose to share it from Settings, and you decide where it goes.

Children

Sheaf is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact
